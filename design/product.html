The Art of the Three-Day Fermentation

Our Ijebu Gari isn't just a staple; it's a labor of time. Sourced from the mineral-rich soils of Ogun State, the cassava is harvested at peak maturity. It is then peeled, grated, and fermented for exactly 72 hours.

This critical window allows the natural starches to transform, developing that signature 'Ijebu' sharpness—a tart, crisp profile that enthusiasts across the globe seek out.

Finally, it is dry-fried in large iron pans over controlled wood fires, achieving a consistent, sand-like texture that remains crunchy even when soaked.
